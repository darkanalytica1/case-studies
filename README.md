# Field Research

Short, original case studies in intelligence, RF, security and unmanned systems. Written from public sources, for learning and for giving something back to the community.

These are independent research notes. They are not affiliated with, or derived from, any employer, client or engagement. They use only public, open sources, and they cite them. Where a topic touches operational detail, the note stays at the level of publicly documented concepts.

## Method

Each note follows the same short shape: the question, what the public sources actually say, a plain-language analysis, and why it matters. No hype, no unsourced claims, one idea per note.
